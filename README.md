Description of Each Project:

CAPSTONE_LSTM: Capstone Report documenting workflow, modules and system preformance of the Residential/Business/Industrial Load Forecasting webapp hosted on Azure.

Clock Gen: The PLL Loop required for the SAR ADC

Comparator: The Comparator Circuit required for the decision making aspect of the SAR ADC

Sample and Hold: The circuit required to convert analog signals into digital signals

SAR: The circuitry that is used in combination with the comparator to convert the input from the sample and hold into one of the digital levels

SAR ADC: Combination of the components above
